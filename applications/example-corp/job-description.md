## Posting body (captured verbatim)

Example Corp is hiring a Data Analyst Intern into Operations Analytics for a
12-week summer placement in Manchester.

You will work with the operations team to turn messy operational data into
reporting people actually use. Much of what we do is unglamorous: finding out
why a number is wrong, agreeing what it should mean, and then making it
reliable enough that nobody checks it by hand any more.

**What you'll do**
- Build and maintain SQL-backed reporting for depot and route performance.
- Investigate data quality problems and trace them back to their source.
- Work directly with operations staff to agree metric definitions before
  building anything.
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders.

**What we're looking for**
- Currently studying toward a degree in a quantitative discipline.
- Confident with SQL; comfortable with Python for analysis.
- Evidence you can find the real cause of a problem rather than describing
  the symptom.
- Clear communication — we care more about this than about modelling depth.

**Nice to have**
- Experience with a BI tool (Power BI, Tableau, Looker).
- Any exposure to logistics, supply chain or field operations.
